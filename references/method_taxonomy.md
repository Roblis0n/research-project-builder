# Method Taxonomy

Methods should follow data reality.

## Low-risk routes

- Evidence matrix.
- Scoping review or systematic mapping.
- Descriptive statistics.
- Content analysis.
- Small qualitative pilot.
- Data-availability audit.

## Medium-complexity routes

- Survey and regression.
- Mediation/moderation.
- Structural equation modeling.
- Mixed methods.
- External validation.
- Bibliometric analysis.

## High-complexity routes

- Causal inference.
- Longitudinal modeling.
- Machine learning.
- Deep learning.
- Simulation.
- System prototype with evaluation.

## Rule

Use the minimum defensible method first. Upgrade only when data, sample size, labels, measurement validity, and time window justify the upgrade.
