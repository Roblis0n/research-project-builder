# Strategic Decision Interview

Stage 0 asks only questions that change the research route.

## D1. Intended output

Options: paper, proposal, grant, system prototype, model/algorithm, or data-analysis report.

Default: paper/proposal-ready.

Why it matters: the output type determines theory depth, method complexity, evidence standard, writing structure, and MVP size.

## D2. Non-negotiable core

Options: research object, setting, method, theoretical problem, data source, or application goal.

Default: preserve the research object and core problem; redesign setting, method, theory, and data route if needed.

Why it matters: the non-negotiable core determines how the project avoids duplication and what can be sacrificed for execution.

## D3. Data reality

Options: existing private data, public-source collection, interviews/surveys, experiment, no data yet, or public data only.

Default: assume no private data and design a public-data, small-pilot, or traceable-document route first.

Why it matters: data reality determines whether causal inference, machine learning, qualitative work, review/synthesis, simulation, or prototyping is possible.

## D4. Method complexity

Options: review, qualitative interviews, survey/statistics, causal inference, machine learning, deep learning, simulation, or system design.

Default: minimum defensible method first; advanced methods only after data supports them.

Why it matters: method complexity determines sample size, variables, tool stack, time cost, failure risk, and interpretability.

## D5. Innovation-feasibility trade-off

Options: conservative/finishable, robust/publishable, or high-risk/high-novelty.

Default: robust and publishable.

Why it matters: the trade-off determines whether the project follows validation/context transfer, mechanism deepening, or new-data/new-model routes.

## D6. Time window

Options: 1-2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or more than 1 year.

Default: 8-12 week MVP.

Why it matters: the time window determines whether the project can include longitudinal data, experiments, complex modeling, interviews, prototyping, or only topic landing.

## D7. Current stage

Options: topic only, topic plus closest literature, topic plus method outline, full project plan, or later theory/model expansion.

Default: topic plus closest literature plus method outline.

Why it matters: output depth should not exceed evidence maturity.
