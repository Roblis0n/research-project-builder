# Source Priority

Use the strongest available evidence before making topic, gap, method, dataset, benchmark, or novelty judgments.

## Priority order

1. Recent systematic reviews, scoping reviews, evidence maps, guidelines, and standards.
2. Highly similar empirical or modeling studies.
3. Adjacent studies that share object, data, method, theory, or outcome.
4. Dataset, benchmark, tool, and reporting-standard documentation.
5. Grey literature and institutional sources when current status matters.
6. Opinion pieces only when they explain practice context, not as evidence of research coverage.

## Minimum traceability

A usable source should have at least one of: DOI, URL, OpenAlex ID, Semantic Scholar ID, PMID, arXiv ID, or another stable identifier.
